Everyone takes Core Pure (Y420). Your route adds either Mechanics Major and a different minor, Statistics Major and a different minor, or three minors. Check the permitted combinations.

Core Pure (Y420)

  • Proof by Induction

    Core Pure. Use induction to prove results about sums, divisibility, inequalities and matrices. State your base case, the induction hypothesis and what the induction step establishes.

    Question pack (PDF) Worked answers (PDF)

  • Complex Number Arithmetic

    Core Pure. Work in radians and take principal arguments in (−π, π].

    Members only

  • Argand Diagrams and Loci

    Core Pure. For a sketch, label axes, boundary points and whether the boundary is included.

    Members only

  • Complex Powers and Roots

    Core Pure. De Moivre’s theorem, exponential form, roots of unity and trigonometric sums. Show your working and give exact values where appropriate.

    Members only

  • Matrix Arithmetic and Transformations

    Core Pure. Each inner bracket gives a matrix row. Apply matrices to column vectors.

    Members only

  • Matrix Determinants and Systems

    Core Pure. Each inner bracket gives a matrix row. Explain how each row or column operation affects the determinant.

    Members only

  • Polynomial Roots and Finite Sums

    Core Pure. State the symmetric sums you use, and show the surviving first and last terms in telescoping sums.

    Members only

  • Maclaurin Series and Limits

    Core Pure. State a range of validity when requested. Keep terms to sufficient order before dividing.

    Members only

  • Further Calculus

    Core Pure. Volumes of revolution, improper integrals, inverse trigonometric functions and partial fractions. Show your working and give exact values where appropriate.

    Members only

  • Further Vectors

    Core Pure. Lines and planes in three dimensions, intersections, angles and perpendicular distances. Show your working and give exact values where appropriate.

    Members only

  • Polar Coordinates

    Core Pure. Polar conversions, curve features, tangents and enclosed areas. Show your working and give exact values where appropriate.

    Members only

  • Hyperbolic Functions

    Core Pure. Definitions, identities, inverse forms, derivatives and integrals. Show your working and give exact values where appropriate.

    Members only

  • First and Second Order Differential Equations

    Core Pure. Give the general solution before applying initial conditions where useful.

    Members only

  • Coupled Equations and Oscillations

    Core Pure. For motion models, state the positive direction and measure displacement from equilibrium.

    Members only

Mechanics Major (Y421) and Minor (Y431)

  • Dimensional Analysis

    Major and minor. Check dimensional consistency and deduce indices from mass, length and time.

    Members only

  • Equilibrium, Friction and Toppling

    Major and minor. Resolve forces and take moments to model beams, suspended bodies and toppling.

    Members only

  • Work, Energy and Power

    Major and minor. Use work and energy for constant forces, and find the power delivered by a vehicle.

    Members only

  • Momentum and Impulse

    Major and minor. Apply vector momentum and impulse to constant forces, coalescence and separation.

    Members only

  • Direct Collisions and Restitution

    Major and minor. Use conservation of momentum and the coefficient of restitution for impacts along a single line.

    Members only

  • Centres of Mass for Particles and Composite Bodies

    Major and minor. Use mass-weighted positions and known centroids, including for laminas with a removed part.

    Members only

  • Oblique Collisions

    Major only. Resolve impulse and restitution along and perpendicular to the line of impact.

    Members only

  • Circular Motion

    Major only. Analyse radial and tangential acceleration in horizontal and vertical circles.

    Members only

  • Hooke's Law and Elastic Energy

    Major only. Model elastic strings and springs, and connect tension, extension and stored energy.

    Members only

  • Centres of Mass by Integration

    Major only. Integrate to find centroids of laminas and uniform solids of revolution.

    Members only

  • Variable Forces and Simple Harmonic Motion

    Major only. Integrate vector force components and model motion with a restoring force.

    Members only

  • Projectile Paths and Ranges on Slopes

    Major only. Find a Cartesian trajectory and use it to calculate uphill and downhill range.

    Members only

Statistics Major (Y422) and Minor (Y432)

  • Sampling and Experimental Design

    Major and minor. Explain sample size, representativeness and why random selection matters.

    Members only

  • Discrete Random Variables and Expectation

    Major and minor. Calculate probability functions, expectation and variance, including linear combinations.

    Members only

  • Discrete Uniform and Binomial Models

    Major and minor. Recognise suitable models and calculate probabilities, means and variances.

    Members only

  • Geometric Distribution

    Major and minor. Model the trial on which a first success occurs and calculate probabilities or parameters.

    Members only

  • Poisson Distribution

    Major and minor. Model event counts, combine independent Poisson variables and compare count models.

    Members only

  • Correlation and Linear Regression

    Major and minor. Calculate and interpret Pearson and Spearman correlation, least-squares regression and residuals.

    Members only

  • Chi-Squared Tests for Association

    Major and minor. Use expected counts, degrees of freedom and critical values to test association.

    Members only

  • Chi-Squared Goodness of Fit

    Major and minor. Test how well uniform, binomial and Poisson models describe observed frequencies.

    Members only

  • Continuous Random Variables

    Major only. Work with densities and cumulative distribution functions, and find moments and percentiles.

    Members only

  • Normal Models and Sample Means

    Major only. Use Normal linear combinations, interpret diagnostics and apply the central limit theorem.

    Members only

  • Confidence Intervals and Paired Data

    Major only. Construct and interpret Normal and t intervals for a single population mean or paired differences.

    Members only

  • Normal, t and Wilcoxon Tests

    Major only. Select a test for a single population mean or median and interpret the result in context.

    Members only

  • Spreadsheet Simulation

    Major only. Simulate distributions and interpret empirical probabilities and statistics from repeated samples.

    Members only

Modelling with Algorithms

  • Sorting, Packing and Complexity

    Trace quick sort and packing algorithms, count operations and reason about heuristics.

    Members only

  • Graphs, Spanning Trees and Shortest Paths

    Use graph terminology, Kruskal’s and Prim’s algorithms, and Dijkstra’s algorithm on small networks.

    Members only

  • Critical Paths and Resourcing

    Model precedence with activity-on-arc networks, find floats and build schedules.

    Members only

  • Network Flows and Cuts

    Augment flows and use cut capacities to prove maximum flow.

    Members only

  • Linear Programming and Simplex

    Model constraints, find graphical optima and interpret simplex variables.

    Members only

Numerical Methods

  • Errors and Numerical Differentiation

    Track error through calculations and estimate derivatives from nearby values.

    Members only

  • Numerical Equation Solving

    Compare bracketing methods, open iterations and convergence.

    Members only

  • Midpoint, Trapezium and Simpson’s Rules

    Approximate integrals, compare errors and use the relationship between the rules.

    Members only

  • Polynomial Interpolation

    Construct difference tables and interpolate using Newton and Lagrange forms.

    Members only

Extra Pure

  • Recurrence Relations and Steady States

    Solve first- and second-order recurrences, including repeated characteristic roots.

    Members only

  • Binary Operations and Groups

    Work with group axioms, cyclic groups, element orders and subgroups.

    Members only

  • Eigenvalues and Cayley–Hamilton

    Find eigendirections, diagonalise matrices and use a characteristic equation to simplify matrix calculations.

    Members only

  • Surfaces and Partial Derivatives

    Find contours, sections, stationary points, tangent planes and normal lines.

    Members only

Further Pure with Technology

  • Curve Investigations

    Explore families of curves with graphing software or CAS and justify observations algebraically.

    Members only

  • Exploring Differential Equations

    Compare tangent fields, numerical approximations and exact solutions.

    Members only

  • Number Theory with Programming

    Trace and design short programs, use modular arithmetic and apply number theory results.

    Members only
