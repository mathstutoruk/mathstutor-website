Number and Algebra

  • Counting and Binomial Series

    Count arrangements and selections, then expand expressions with negative and fractional powers. Try this pack without a calculator.

    Question pack (PDF) Worked answers (PDF)

  • Partial Fractions and Linear Systems

    Resolve a rational expression into two fractions and use elimination to classify linear systems. Try this pack without a calculator.

    Members only

  • Mathematical Proof

    Practise induction, contradiction and counterexamples. Try this pack without a calculator.

    Members only

  • Complex Number Forms

    Convert between Cartesian, polar and Euler forms, and interpret complex multiplication geometrically. Try this pack without a calculator.

    Members only

  • Complex Roots and De Moivre's Theorem

    Use De Moivre’s theorem and conjugate roots to solve polynomial equations. Try this pack without a calculator.

    Members only

Functions

  • Polynomial Factors and Roots

    Use the factor and remainder theorems and connect polynomial coefficients to roots. Try this pack without a calculator.

    Members only

  • Rational Functions and Asymptotes

    Identify vertical, horizontal and oblique asymptotes, and use them to sketch rational graphs. Try this pack without a calculator.

    Members only

  • Inverse Functions and Symmetry

    Find inverse functions with the correct domain and range, and use even and odd symmetry. Try this pack without a calculator.

    Members only

  • Modulus and Graph Transformations

    Compare graph transformations and solve inequalities. Use a graphing calculator for the final question.

    Members only

Geometry and Trigonometry

  • Higher Trigonometric Identities

    Use reciprocal and inverse trigonometric functions, compound angles and symmetry. Work in radians without a calculator.

    Members only

  • Vectors and Scalar Products

    Find displacements, use unit vectors and apply the scalar product to angles and geometric proofs. Give exact results where possible.

    Members only

  • Vector Lines and Intersections

    Convert line equations between forms and distinguish intersecting, parallel and skew lines. Give exact results where possible.

    Members only

  • Cross Products and Planes

    Use the vector product to find areas and plane normals, then solve intersections and angles involving planes. Give exact results where possible.

    Members only

Statistics and Probability

  • Bayes’ Theorem and Conditional Probability

    Work backwards from observed outcomes using two or three possible causes. Non-calculator practice.

    Members only

  • Discrete Expectation and Variance

    Calculate moments, set a fair price and analyse linear transformations. Non-calculator practice.

    Members only

  • Continuous Probability Densities

    Normalise densities and find interval probabilities, measures of centre and variance. Non-calculator practice.

    Members only

  • Binomial and Normal Models

    Set up tail probabilities and solve inverse distribution problems. Graphic display calculator practice.

    Members only

Calculus

  • Limits and Higher Derivatives

    Work from first principles and use repeated differentiation or l’Hôpital’s rule. Non-calculator practice.

    Members only

  • Maclaurin Series

    Build expansions by substitution, products, integration and a differential equation. Non-calculator practice.

    Members only

  • Implicit Differentiation and Rates

    Find tangents, solve related rates problems and compare stationary values with endpoints. Non-calculator practice.

    Members only

  • Advanced Integration Techniques

    Choose substitution, parts, partial fractions or an inverse trigonometric form. Non-calculator practice.

    Members only

  • Areas and Volumes of Revolution

    Set up disk and washer integrals about either axis. Non-calculator practice.

    Members only

  • First-Order Differential Equations

    Solve by separation, substitution, integrating factors and Euler’s method for the 2026–2028 syllabus.

    Members only

Paper 3 Problem Solving

  • Paper 3: Calibrating a Bottle

    Find the profile of a bottle, calibrate its fill line and test a manufacturing constraint. Graphic display calculator practice.

    Members only

  • Paper 3: Choosing a Sensor Threshold

    Combine Bayes’ theorem, binomial probabilities and expected cost to choose a fault warning rule. Graphic display calculator practice.

    Members only
