Who are the classes for?

Group classes suit students who want regular support with their course, whether they’re getting to grips with the basics or preparing for exams.

They offer a chance to hear how other students approach a question and to talk through the steps you find difficult.

How a lesson works

  1. Learn the method. I explain the topic and work through an example on the shared whiteboard.
  2. Try it yourself. You put the method into practice with questions and can ask for help if you get stuck.
  3. Review the working. We compare approaches, fix common mistakes and look at where exam marks are earned.

Classes run on Zoom. You can follow along with pen and paper.
