Pure Core (Y540 and Y541)

  • Proof by Induction

    Induction proofs involving sums, recurrences, divisibility, inequalities and matrix powers.

    Question pack (PDF) Worked answers (PDF)

  • Complex Number Arithmetic

    Cartesian and modulus-argument forms, conjugates, and quadratic and quartic equations.

    Members only

  • Argand Diagrams and Loci

    Circles, rays, perpendicular bisectors and shaded regions in the Argand plane.

    Members only

  • Complex Powers and Roots

    De Moivre’s theorem, exponential form, roots of unity and trigonometric sums.

    Members only

  • Matrix Arithmetic and Transformations

    Matrix operations, 2D and 3D transformations, invariant points and lines, and area scales.

    Members only

  • Matrix Determinants and Systems

    3×3 inverses, determinant factorisation, singularity and intersections of planes.

    Members only

  • Polynomial Roots and Finite Sums

    Roots and coefficients, transformed roots, formulae for sums and telescoping.

    Members only

  • Maclaurin Series and Limits

    Series from derivatives, standard expansions, the general term and limits.

    Members only

  • Further Calculus

    Volumes of revolution about either axis, improper integrals, mean values, inverse trig functions and partial fractions.

    Members only

  • Further Vectors

    Lines and planes in three dimensions, intersections, angles and perpendicular distances.

    Members only

  • Polar Coordinates

    Polar and Cartesian coordinates, symmetry, significant curve features and enclosed areas.

    Members only

  • Hyperbolic Functions

    Definitions, identities, inverse forms, derivatives and integrals.

    Members only

  • First and Second Order Differential Equations

    Integrating factors, auxiliary equations and particular integrals.

    Members only

  • Coupled Equations and Oscillations

    Coupled systems, Hooke’s law, simple harmonic motion and models with damping.

    Members only

Statistics (Y542)

  • Probability and Combinatorics

    Permutations, combinations, arrangements with repetitions and selections.

    Members only

  • Discrete Random Variables and Expectation

    Probability tables and functions, expectation, variance, transformations and the discrete uniform distribution.

    Members only

  • Geometric Distribution

    First-success probabilities and tails, conditions, mean and variance.

    Members only

  • Poisson Distribution

    Poisson modelling assumptions, rate scaling, variance and sums of independent counts.

    Members only

  • Continuous Random Variables

    Densities, distribution functions, percentiles, moments and transformations.

    Members only

  • The Exponential Distribution

    Waiting-time models, the density and cumulative distribution, probabilities and derivation of moments.

    Members only

  • Linear Combinations of Random Variables

    Means, variances and linear combinations of independent Normal random variables.

    Members only

  • Normal Tests and Confidence Intervals

    Sample means, the central limit theorem, unbiased variance, Normal tests and confidence intervals.

    Members only

  • Chi-squared Tests for Association

    Contingency tables, expected frequencies, degrees of freedom, sources of association and Yates’ correction.

    Members only

  • Chi-squared Goodness of Fit

    Expected frequencies, pooling categories, fitting distributions and interpreting conclusions.

    Members only

  • Sign and Wilcoxon Tests

    One-sample and paired sign and signed-rank tests, rank-sum tests, and continuity correction.

    Members only

  • Correlation and Linear Regression

    Pearson and Spearman coefficients, tests for association, least squares, residuals and coding.

    Members only

Mechanics (Y543)

  • Dimensional Analysis

    Dimensions, homogeneous equations and deriving powers in physical models.

    Members only

  • Momentum and Impulse

    Conservation of vector momentum, impulse from constant and variable forces, and energy changes.

    Members only

  • Collisions and Restitution

    Restitution in direct collisions, successive impacts and oblique impacts against a fixed smooth surface.

    Members only

  • Work, Energy, Power and Elasticity

    Work by constant and variable forces, conservation of energy, power, Hooke’s law and elastic potential energy.

    Members only

  • Circular Motion

    Angular motion, conical pendulums, vertical circles, radial and tangential acceleration, and detachment.

    Members only

  • Centres of Mass

    Discrete systems, composite bodies, uniform laminas and solids of revolution.

    Members only

  • Rigid Body Equilibrium and Toppling

    Moments and support reactions, suspension, hinge forces, and comparing sliding and toppling.

    Members only

  • Variable Force Dynamics

    Solve linear motion models with a = dv/dt or a = v dv/dx, by separation or an integrating factor.

    Members only

Discrete Mathematics (Y544)

  • Sets, Counting and the Pigeonhole Principle

    Classifying problems, partitions, arrangements and guaranteed repetitions.

    Members only

  • Graph Theory

    Euler trails, Hamiltonian cycles, planarity, adjacency matrices and graph isomorphism.

    Members only

  • Sorting, Packing and Run Time

    Bubble, shuttle and quick sort, packing heuristics, tracing and orders of growth.

    Members only

  • Network Algorithms

    Dijkstra, Prim, Kruskal, travelling salesperson bounds and route inspection.

    Members only

  • Activity Networks and Scheduling

    Activity on arc, dummy activities, event times, floats and worker schedules.

    Members only

  • Graphical Linear Programming

    Formulate constraints, find feasible vertices and solve integer problems.

    Members only

  • The Simplex Algorithm

    Slack variables, ratio tests, pivots, objective rows and interpreting tableaus.

    Members only

  • Zero-sum Games

    Pay-off matrices, play-safe strategies, saddle points, dominance, graphical mixing and linear programmes.

    Members only

Additional Pure Mathematics (Y545)

  • Recurrence Relations and Steady States

    Closed forms, first and second order recurrence equations and convergence.

    Members only

  • Number Theory

    Number bases, the Euclidean algorithm, congruences, the Chinese remainder theorem and Fermat’s little theorem.

    Members only

  • Binary Operations and Groups

    Cayley tables, modular arithmetic, inverses, cyclic groups, symmetry and Lagrange’s theorem.

    Members only

  • Vector Products and Triple Products

    Cross products, scalar triple products and applications to areas, volumes and geometry.

    Members only

  • Surfaces and Partial Differentiation

    Contours, cross-sections, first and second partial derivatives, and classifying stationary points.

    Members only

  • Arc Length, Surfaces and Reduction

    Cartesian and parametric arc lengths, surfaces of revolution, reduction formulae and improper limits.

    Members only
